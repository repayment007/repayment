FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

How long does an average case take to complete?
Although our experienced team will look to complete your case as soon as possible, each case is unique. On average this process takes from 3 months to a year.
What is the cost of pursuing a case?
Repayment provides the tools you need to pursue your case in the form of a detailed Investigation Report that includes a suggested Action Plan. Such a report costs $3,500, with an additional $750 per transaction. Our Full Support Package is $10,000 flat.
Why don't you charge at the end of the process only?
Each case requires a significant amount of man-hours to investigate, process, and bring to a successful conclusion. We charge a fixed fee to cover the costs of producing the investigation report, so payment must be arranged prior to the start of the investigation.
How do I know Repayment isn't a scam?
We believe a question like that is best answered by our clients. We invite you to check the hundreds of verified reviews to confirm we are a legitimate and reputable organization. We are also registered with the American Ministry of Justice, and our staff will happily forward relevant credentials.
Will your actions lead to taking scammers to court?
Our services do not include court representation. Our methods primarily involve Alternative Dispute Resolution (ADR) outside of court — dealing with banks and relevant agencies that made the relevant transactions. We've found this is a far more effective and faster means of securing fund recovery.
Does it matter where I am based?
It doesn't matter where you are based. Our team can help you get your money back from anywhere in the world. As long as you have the relevant documentation, we can help you regardless of your location.
Do all cases end with a successful outcome?
Unfortunately, no. For example, some scams involve cash transfers that rarely have a successful outcome. We are transparent about this — if we believe you cannot retrieve your money, we will tell you immediately during the free consultation. Our 14-day refund policy also protects you.
